The Lofi Desk

The most literal reading of the brief: the binder is a real object on a real desk in a warm, inhabited room — plants at the edge, a mug going cold, honeyed window light by day and a lamp pool by night. Chrome floats like a soft shelf above the scene; everything is rounded, grained, and tactile. It fits because the product's ritual (open the cover, turn the pages, sleeve the card) already belongs on a desk like this one.

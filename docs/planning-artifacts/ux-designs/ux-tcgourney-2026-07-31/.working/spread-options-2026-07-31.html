A

Option A — "The Purist's Binder"

Both pages keep the strict 3×3; every pocket is a sleeve and every Slot is visible, Filled or not. Entries flow in Pokédex order with their Slots contiguous, simply wrapping wherever the grid runs out — Venusaur crosses the spine mid-Entry. The chronicle is carried entirely by fill-state and Rank labels: dense, honest, zero theater.

B

Option B — "The Michi Spread"

Each page is composed, not filled: the Entry's highest-Rank Slot takes a 2×2 hero pocket, its supporting Slots orbit it, and empty paper is left on purpose. The whole spread reads as one evolution panel — Bulbasaur → Ivysaur → Venusaur. Fewer Slots per page means a longer binder, but every spread is a display piece.

C

Option C — "Grid with Hero Moments"

The 3×3 rhythm from Option A is the baseline, broken only at promoted beats: a Region opens on a chapter title page, and any Slot of the top Ranks — Special Illustration Rare, Hyper Rare — earns a center-stage hero pocket. Evolution lines get quiet panel framing inside the grid. Michi curation where the story peaks; purist grid everywhere else.

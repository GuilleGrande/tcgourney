The Watercolor Chronicle

The Orca card's watercolor language becomes the interface itself: pages are cold-press paper with pigment washes bleeding at the corners, cards are mounted specimens held by photo corners, and every label reads like a line from a field guide. Quiet, literary, museum-calm — the chronicle register made visible. It fits because the product's own vision statement calls the binder "a retelling," and a retelling deserves a book.

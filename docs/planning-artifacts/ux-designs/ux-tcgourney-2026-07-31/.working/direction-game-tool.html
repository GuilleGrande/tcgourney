The Trainer's Tool

This is the "tool for a game" turned all the way up: crisp Nintendo-adjacent menu chrome — chunky pills that press when you touch them, a confident flame accent, a segmented progress meter, a button-hint rail — wrapped around soft, warm binder pages. The cozy lives in the butter-and-wood palette and the card content; the chrome stays sharp, snappy, and satisfying. It fits because the collection IS a game, and its controls should feel as good as its pages look.

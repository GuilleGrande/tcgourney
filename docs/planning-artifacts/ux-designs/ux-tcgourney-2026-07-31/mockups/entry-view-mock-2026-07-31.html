Explainer

In the Hidden Village, Bulbasaur stood watch over abandoned and injured Pokémon, turning every traveler away from the bridge. Only after Ash helped defend the village did it agree to battle him — Pikachu wore it down, the Poké Ball closed, and the village's guardian chose the road. Every Entry begins here: the ⚫ base Slot is the Catch itself.

Kanto — Chapter I, and the Trophy Shelf

Every Region opens with a full-page title card: one composition across the whole 3×3 page. The landscape wash spans all nine frames — sky above, hills through the middle, meadow below — while every piece of content keeps to its card frame. The chapter carries its name in the field-guide display voice, its emblem in the throne's ring language, its progress line, and the badge trophy shelf: the region's seven milestone-type summits as coins — earned ones lit in honey, unearned ones waiting as quiet debossed silhouettes.

3 · New design territory documented here

Badge coin language (fills the DESIGN.md Badge gap): badges are circular coins — trophy objects, exempt from the rounded-rect chrome rule the way binder rings and fairy lights are (the pill ban remains a chrome rule). Earned = honey metal: radial honey (#d29a3a day / #e0a94e night) deepening to honey-text #8a6410, espresso 2px ring, cream inner ring, glyph embossed in espresso, soft breathing honey halo (rgba(210,154,58,.55)). Unearned = blind-debossed silhouette: ink-tinted face rgba(74,57,43,.07), dashed sleeve-rim ring, glyph at 32% ink, no halo. No ember on badges, ever — trophies are not actions. Glyphs are placeholders: ⚫ Catch, ⚔ Opponent, ⚑ Teammate, ☄ Encounter, ♥ Bond, ♛ Glory, ↺ Loyal. Shelf: 1/3/3 across the bottom row — The Catch enlarged as the base summit on a honey ledge; star summits in shelf rows. Composition: solo centered page; landscape wash spans all nine frames as sky/hills/meadow bands; dashed honey route passes behind the name panel; content panels are translucent cream. Emblem reuses the throne's ink–cream–honey ring. Badge-earned rule shown per memlog assumption #53 — verify against the rank engine before build.

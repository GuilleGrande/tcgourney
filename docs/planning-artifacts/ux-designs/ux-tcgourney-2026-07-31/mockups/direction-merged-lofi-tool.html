Lofi World, Trainer Chrome

The Lofi Desk's world, whole: an inhabited room with a window behind the desk, honeyed light by day and moon-plus-lamp by night, wood grain, plants, a mug going cold, a leather binder of cream paper. Inside that world, every control speaks the Trainer's Tool language: thick espresso borders, hard press-shadows that sink on hover, one confident accent — but every corner is a rounded rectangle (8–12px), never a capsule.

The one accent — Ember #d95f36 (night #ff8257). The Trainer's Tool's flame, annealed toward the Lofi Desk's terracotta pot: fired clay in honeyed light. It reads as part of the room — the pot on the shelf, the pencil on the desk — so the chrome's confidence never breaks the coziness. Ember marks only what is interactive or chased: search glyph, active chips, the CHASING ghost, the current ladder rung, the sleeve button. Rank labels stay quiet honey (#d29a3a) and ink.

In the Hidden Village, Bulbasaur stood watch over abandoned and injured Pokémon, turning every traveler away from the bridge. Only after Ash helped defend the village did it agree to battle him — Pikachu wore it down, the Poké Ball closed, and the village's guardian chose the road.

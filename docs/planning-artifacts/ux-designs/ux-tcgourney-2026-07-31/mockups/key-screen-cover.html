The Cover — the binder at rest

What the Collector sees every single time the app opens: the closed leather binder on the wood desk, the big six-pane window pouring light behind it. Pure and quiet — a beautiful object with no data on it. No progress, no counts, no chrome. The title is blind-embossed into the leather; the only invitation is the object itself. Clicking anywhere on the binder begins the opening ritual.

1 · The Cover, day — first frame on app open (hover the binder: it lifts and the light gathers)
Ash's Journey

Click anywhere on the binder and the opening ritual begins — the cover lifts and the first spread breathes open; with reduced motion set, it simply cuts to the open page. On hover (or keyboard focus) the binder rises 8px and a honeyed halo gathers around it — the room's own light saying open me. No search bar, no chips, no meter: the tool arrives with the open binder. The cover carries only its embossed title, a stitched frame, and a quiet leather medallion.

2 · Night strip — the same object after dark (lamp lit, moonlit window)
Ash's Journey

Night: the moon takes the window, the lamp pools warm light over the desk, and the leather deepens to its night tone (#4b3221) — the same quiet object, waiting. The hover halo warms toward the lamp (rgba of #ffbe69). The ritual is identical day or night.

3 · New design territory documented here

Cover embossing: blind deboss — leather-on-leather, never foil or chrome. Title ink is the leather darkened (color-mix leather 52% + black); the deboss reads through a paired text-shadow: a raised lip below (rgba(255,240,210,.16)) and a sunk face above (rgba(0,0,0,.55)). Furniture: the existing dashed stitch token as an inset frame, one debossed rule, and a debossed circular medallion (band + hub — a quiet ball silhouette, decorative only). Chrome is absent at the cover — the persistent HUD arrives with the open binder (extends decision #49; confirm against #45). Invitation affordance: whole binder = target; hover/focus lifts 8px with a honeyed halo; keyboard focus adds the ember ring (the one act-here on this screen). Reduced motion keeps the halo, drops the lift. The Hunt sticky-note is omitted here — it carries data and the cover is pure.

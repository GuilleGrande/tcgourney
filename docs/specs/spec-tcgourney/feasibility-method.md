# Completion Feasibility — Method

Whether the full Binder is completable cannot be known until charting closes and the Slots are counted. The question is carried deliberately; the **method** for answering it is fixed now.

## Method

- Total Slot count is the sum over Entries: an Entry with N Stars contributes N Slots, plus the ⚫ Slot when Caught.
- **Difficulty is not proportional to Slot count.** It concentrates almost entirely in the top Ranks (Ultra Rare and above). A roster of a thousand mostly-low-rank Slots is a cheaper chase than one with thirty ceiling Slots.
- The feasibility metric is therefore **the distribution of Entries by Star count** — computable from Milestone data alone, before a single card is priced.

## Adjustment levers, if infeasible

1. Raise the Star thresholds for the top Ranks.
2. Cap Slots per Entry.
3. Redefine completion against the interim milestones: every Entry holding at least one Card, every ⚫ base Slot filled, a Region completed at a time.

The interim milestones are not consolation prizes — they are progress views CAP-16 must be able to express.

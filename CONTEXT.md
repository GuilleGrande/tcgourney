# Ash's Journey — Domain Context

The ubiquitous language for a planner that retraces Ash Ketchum's anime journey. Every Pokémon that *mattered* to the tale earns places in a single Binder, and the rarity of card each place demands is set by how many story Milestones that Pokémon lived through.

## The Binder

**Binder**:
The complete, finite collection — the whole journey retraced. Its roster is closed: every Pokémon that will ever belong has already earned its place.
_Avoid_: Album, Pokédex, collection

**Roster**:
The set of Pokémon that earned a place in the Binder, assembled from sub-rosters (the catch list, the Glory roster, the Loyal roster, the Encounters, Team Rocket).
_Avoid_: List, dex

**Entry**:
One Pokémon at one evolution stage as it sits in the Binder — Charmander, Charmeleon, and Charizard are three Entries. An Entry owns a Rank and the Slots beneath it.
_Avoid_: Row; Card (a Card fills a Slot, it is not the Entry)

**Slot**:
A single fillable place in the Binder: one Entry at one Rank. An Entry of Rank N has one Slot per rank rung up to N, plus a Common/Uncommon Slot only when the Pokémon was Caught. Any Card whose Rarity maps to the Slot's Rank may fill it.
_Avoid_: Spot, cell, position

## The Ranks

The Rank ladder, low to high: **Common/Uncommon (⚫)** · **Rare (⭐)** · **Double Rare (⭐⭐)** · **Ultra Rare (⭐⭐⭐)** · **Illustration Rare (⭐⭐⭐⭐)** · **Special Illustration Rare (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)** · **Hyper Rare (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)**.

**Rank**:
The rarity tier a Slot demands. An Entry's Rank is the highest rung it reached, set by its Star count.
_Avoid_: Tier, grade; Rarity (a Card's printed attribute — Rank is ours)

**Star**:
The unit of rank. An Entry earns one Star per distinct star-granting Milestone it holds. Zero Stars is the ⚫ base (Common/Uncommon); six Stars is the ceiling (Hyper Rare).
_Avoid_: Token, point, pip

## The Milestones

**Milestone**:
A story achievement that grants an Entry its place or a Star. Seven exist — the Catch (the ⚫ base) and the six star-granting ones below. Each type is earned at most once per Entry.
_Avoid_: Achievement, gate, badge

**Catch**:
Ash caught the Pokémon. Grants the ⚫ base and is the only Milestone that unlocks the Common/Uncommon Slot. Not required to belong — an uncaught Pokémon can still earn Stars (see Meowth).
_Avoid_: Capture, own

**Opponent**:
The Pokémon faced Ash as an opponent in Official Competition. +1 Star.
_Avoid_: Rival, enemy

**Teammate**:
The Pokémon fought on Ash's side in Official Competition. +1 Star.
_Avoid_: Party member (party membership alone is not this Milestone — competition is)

**Encounter**:
A wild Pokémon Ash never owned whose path crossed his in a journey-shaping moment; the chasable path for Legendaries. +1 Star.
_Avoid_: Sighting, meeting

**Bond**:
A memorable emotional tie between Ash and the Pokémon, ownership aside — why Meowth belongs. Reaches its height in a Farewell. +1 Star.
_Avoid_: Friendship, relationship

**Glory**:
The Pokémon was on the active roster the day Ash won a Trophy. +1 Star.
_Avoid_: Victory, win

**Loyal**:
The Pokémon earned a Milestone in a Region beyond its original one — the companion who came back. Never earned alone, so it always rides with at least one other Star. +1 Star.
_Avoid_: Returning, veteran

## Story Concepts

**Appearance**:
One instance of a species in the journey — Ash's own, a rival's, or a wild one — that contributes Milestones to that species' Entry. Every Appearance feeds the same Slot.
_Avoid_: Instance, occurrence, sighting

**Evolution Line**:
The ordered chain of an Entry's forms (Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard). Milestones travel forward up the line only; every later form inherits all earlier Stars and never slides back.
_Avoid_: Evolution chain; Family (family can branch — a Line is the walked path)

**Farewell**:
The moment Ash released or left a Pokémon behind. The summit of a Bond, never a demotion; it crowns the Bond Star rather than granting one of its own.
_Avoid_: Release, goodbye, departure

**Official Competition**:
A League, tournament, Contest, or World Coronation stage — the recorded proving grounds that qualify the Opponent and Teammate Milestones.
_Avoid_: Battle (a casual battle does not count), match

**Trophy**:
An official League or Championship won and written into the record — Orange League, Alola League, the World Championship. Fixes the Glory roster.
_Avoid_: Prize, cup, title

**Region**:
A leg of the journey (Kanto, Johto, … Galar). An Entry's *original* Region is where it first entered Ash's story; earning a Milestone in any later Region is what makes it Loyal.
_Avoid_: Generation, arc

## Cards & the Chase

**Card**:
A real, printed Pokémon TCG card from any set or era. It fills a Slot when its Rarity maps to the Slot's Rank.
_Avoid_: Print, copy

**Rarity**:
A Card's printed rarity as the TCG defines it (Rare Holo, ex, GX, V, Illustration Rare, …). Distinct from Rank — the Rarity Translation Map connects the two.
_Avoid_: Rank (ours), grade

**Rarity Translation Map**:
The mapping from real-set Rarities, across all eras, onto the seven Ranks — so a Card from any set can find the Slot it fits.
_Avoid_: Rarity chart, conversion table

**The Chase**:
The act of hunting Cards to fill empty Slots.
_Avoid_: Hunt, collecting
